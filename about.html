Since Day One
Our Story

Built around one material science.

Our product range is built entirely around fluoropolymer lining technology — PTFE, PFA and FEP — applied through a precision paste-extruding lining process onto engineered carbon steel, stainless steel or cast bodies.

Every product in the range, from a single lined spool to a fully lined ball valve, is designed so the process fluid only ever touches inert fluoropolymer, never the structural metal body.

Vision

The trusted standard in corrosion-resistant flow

To be the reference point process engineers reach for whenever a line has to survive an aggressive chemical duty.

Mission

Engineer failure out of the process line

To manufacture fluoropolymer lined components that are dimensionally precise, chemically inert and independently pressure & spark tested — every time.
