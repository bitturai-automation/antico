About Us

Engineered so the metal never touches the process.

Antico Flow System designs and manufactures fluoropolymer lined pipes, fittings, valves and custom flow components for industries where a single leak isn't an option.

Our Story

Built around one material science.

Our product range is built entirely around fluoropolymer lining technology — PTFE, PFA and FEP — applied through a precision paste-extruding lining process onto engineered carbon steel, stainless steel or cast bodies.

Every product in the range, from a single lined spool to a fully lined ball valve, is designed so the process fluid only ever touches inert fluoropolymer, never the structural metal body. It's a narrow focus, kept deliberately — one lining discipline, applied consistently across 43 components.
