FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from process engineers and procurement teams. Can't find what you're looking for?

Contact Our Team
What linings do you offer and how do I choose the right one?+

We manufacture PTFE, PFA and FEP linings, each suited to different chemical, temperature and permeation requirements. Share your fluid and process duty conditions with our applications team and we'll recommend the right lining for your service.

What size range and pressure ratings are available?+

Our standard range covers NB ½″ to 24″ up to ASME/ANSI B16.5 Class 150, manufactured to documented industry standards. Larger sizes and higher ratings can be discussed for special design products.

Do you provide custom or special-design fabrication?+

Yes. Beyond our standard catalogue, we engineer custom headers, dip pipes, manifolds and other fabricated designs to your exact process drawing and duty conditions.

What testing and quality documentation do you provide?+

Every finished component undergoes 100% dimensional, spark and hydro/pneumatic pressure testing. Test certificates and QA documentation, including EN 10204 3.1 where required, are available on request.

What are typical lead times for standard and custom orders?+

Lead times depend on size, lining and quantity. Standard catalogue items generally ship faster than custom fabricated designs — share your requirement with our team for a firm delivery schedule.

Which industries and applications do you serve?+

We supply corrosion-resistant flow components to chemical processing, pharmaceutical, petrochemical, fertilizer and specialty chemical industries, and other applications handling aggressive or high-purity fluids.
