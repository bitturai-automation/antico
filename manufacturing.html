Built around the
details you don't see.

Two details that don't always show up on a spec sheet — but matter every day the fitting is in service.

VENT

Vent Holes

Drilled into the steel body of every lined fitting to relieve back pressure between the metal housing and the liner, allow leak detection during pressure testing, and flag corrosion early.

Spools under 500mm carry a single 3mm vent hole; spools over 500mm carry two, spaced ~150mm from each end.
EARTHING

Electrical Continuity

Established via conductors attached to earthing lugs — welded centrally for fittings and spools under 500mm, or ~150mm from the rear of each flange for spools over 500mm.

Type A or B earthing lugs in SS 304 or SS 316 are available on request.
