WHO WE ARE

When corrosion stops production, engineering starts.

Antico Flow System is based in Vadodara, Gujarat, and supplies fluoropolymer-lined flow systems to the chemical and process industries, where a single leak can stop a plant.

We work from your line drawings, fluid data and duty conditions. Our engineers prepare isometric drawings and a complete fitting list for sign-off. Components are then machined, lined and fabricated in-house to specification.

Every lined component is inspected before dispatch, and our team stays available for installation queries after delivery.
