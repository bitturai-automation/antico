OVERVIEW

How Antico supports petrochemical

Petrochemical units combine corrosive streams with pressure, temperature swings and vibration. Piping has to hold its integrity across all of them.

Antico lined systems pair a steel shell for mechanical strength with a fluoropolymer lining for chemical resistance, and expansion bellows take up movement in the line.

CHALLENGES

What this industry needs from its flow system

01

Pressure and temperature

Operating conditions change and pipework must cope with both.

02

Thermal expansion

Bellows absorb expansion and vibration to protect flanges and equipment.

03

Corrosive by-products

Acidic and caustic streams attack unprotected pipework over time.

04

Shutdown cost

Downtime in continuous units is expensive, so reliability matters most.
