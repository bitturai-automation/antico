01Overview

Antico Flow System ("Antico", "we", "us") is based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. We manufacture PTFE, PFA and FEP lined pipes, fittings, valves, bellows and custom equipment for chemical and process industries.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the website or submitting a request to us, you agree to the practices described here.

02Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and run the website.

Information you give us

  • Quote and enquiry forms: your name, company, email address, phone number, industry, products of interest, media or chemical, temperature and pressure, size range and quantity, lining preference, and any message or project details you write.
  • Direct communication: anything you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp, including drawings or fluid data you choose to share.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Small amounts of data stored in your browser (for example, your preferences for this site).

03How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and technical enquiries, and to recommend suitable linings and components.
  • To prepare drawings, offers, order confirmations and after-sales support.
  • To send documents you ask for, such as our product catalogue or certificates.
  • To keep the website secure, understand how it is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

04Sharing your information

We share information only where it is needed to serve you or required by law:

  • Service providers who help us run the website, email, hosting or analytics, and who may only use the data for those services.
  • Logistics and business partners where needed to fulfil an order you have placed.
  • Authorities when we are legally required to disclose information, or to protect our rights, safety or property.
  • A successor business if Antico is ever merged with or acquired by another company.

05Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use cookies and browser storage to keep it working properly and to understand how visitors use it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this section to name them.

06How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and quote records for as long as needed to respond, carry out any resulting order and handle warranty or support questions, and for the period required by applicable law. After that, we delete or anonymise the information.

07Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive information, such as passwords or payment card details, through the website forms.

08Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop sending you marketing messages, or withdraw consent you have given.

To make a request, email mkt.anticoflowsystem@gmail.com from the address you used to contact us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

09Third-party websites

This website may link to other websites, including maps, social networks or supplier pages. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their policies before sharing information.

10Children

Our website and products are intended for businesses and industrial users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
