PTFE Gasket & Envelope Gasket

PTFE NB 1/2" – NB 16"
Request a Quote Download Technical Data

Product Overview

Flange gaskets in PTFE envelope (metallic/non-metallic insert) or solid PTFE form. Built on Antico's fluoropolymer lining process, this component is manufactured to NB 1/2" to NB 16" and lined in PTFE depending on the size and chemical service required.

Key Benefits

  • Fluoropolymer lining selected for chemical compatibility with your process fluid
  • Manufactured to documented dimensional tolerances
  • 100% spark tested and pressure tested before dispatch
