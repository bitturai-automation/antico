Product Overview

Lined Y-strainer protecting downstream valves and instruments from particulates. Built on Antico's fluoropolymer lining process, this component is manufactured to ASME/ANSI B16.5 Class 150 and lined in PFA, FEP depending on the size and chemical service required.

Key Benefits

  • Fluoropolymer lining selected for chemical compatibility with your process fluid
  • Manufactured to documented dimensional tolerances
  • 100% spark tested and pressure tested before dispatch
